How much can you spend on customer acquisition?

I connect your media spend to orders and repeat purchases. I start with one question: three, six or twelve months in, how much margin has a new customer generated after acquisition costs?

I help you decide which budgets to increase, cut or test. You get a recommendation backed by numbers, along with the assumptions that could change it. I then work with your team and agency to put the decision into practice and check its impact on margin.
